## 🧠 Context to carry forward

Two places where the route actually built **differs from the file header's sketch**, and the header
was not updated (deliberately — it's the original plan, the code is the truth):

1. **The circle lemma is a cubic recursion, not a mod-1 argument.** The header proposes
   `times_three_orbit` on fractional parts. Don't. `Re(w³) = 4(Re w)³ − 3‖w‖²(Re w)` plus
   normalising by `‖w‖^{3ʲ}` gives `c_{j+1} = 4c_j³ − 3c_j`, and negativity of every iterate forces
   `c_0 = −1` by `c_j+1 ≥ 4ʲ(c_0+1)`. No trig, no `Complex.arg`.
2. **The complex-case endgame was genuinely open in the header** ("`β^N ∈ ℚ(β)` has a conjugate of
   multiplicity 2 while its degree is 1 or 3"). That phrasing wants root-set transport / embeddings.
   The cheap closure instead eliminates `y` between the *two* trace relations at `N` and `2N`,
   producing the explicit integer quadratic `3x² − 2tx + (t² − 2T) = 0` killed by `x = β^N`; that
   caps `deg β^N ≤ 2`, `u^N ∈ otherConj(β^N)` pins it at 2, and the already-proved
   `not_pisot_two_of_cube` finishes. No Galois machinery anywhere in the file.

Also worth knowing: the `√y log² y` in `exists_prime_short_interval` is **not slack**. I first
budgeted `√y log y` (the shape usually quoted for RH prime gaps) and it loses — Schoenfeld's error
appears at *both* endpoints, so the main term must beat `√y log y`, forcing the extra `log`. The
final contradiction still works because `log(p_k³) ≍ 3N log β` is polynomial in `N` while
`ρ^{N/2}` is exponential.

Unconditional route was checked and rejected: `saito_lemma36C` caps the gap only by `p_k^{63/40}`,
and `p_k^{0.075}` is itself exponential in `N`, so `ρ^{N/2}` need not beat it. RH is genuinely load-bearing.
