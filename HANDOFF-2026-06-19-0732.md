- **The descent.** `hstep (oadd E 1 0) b` (predecessor of `ω^E` at arg `b`) recurses via
  `fundamentalSequence`: E successor (`= some E'`) → peels to `oadd E' ⟨b⟩ (hstep (oadd E' 1 0) b)`
  via Lemma A; E limit (`= inr f`) → recurses on `oadd (f b) 1 0`. Well-founded on `repr E`.
- **The answer** for *arbitrary* NF E is `toONote (b+1) ((b+1)^(evalNat b E) − 1)`, where
  `evalNat b E` = read `repr E`'s CNF as a base-`(b+1)` numeral. For `E = toONote b L`,
  `evalNat b E = bump b L` (proved: `evalNat_toONote`), so the general lemma specializes to the headline.
- **Why the limit case is EASY now:** `evalNat_fundSeq` proves `evalNat b (f b) = evalNat b E`
  (the descent's `b+1` coefficient at index `b` lands the evaluation right back). So limit-case
  IH on `f b` + `evalNat_fundSeq` closes it with NO reconstruction needed.
- **The LONE remaining wall = the successor case's reconstruction:** it produces
  `oadd E' ⟨b⟩ (answer(E'))` and needs this `= answer(E)`. Via `toONote_oadd` + `evalNat_succ`
  (`evalNat E = evalNat E' + 1`, proved) this reduces to **`toONote (b+1) (evalNat b E') = E'`**
  for `E' = pred E`. That reconstruction is FALSE for arbitrary NF E' (e.g. `finite(b+1)`,
  which the descent really does produce as an `f b`!) — it holds only for base-`(b+1)`-CNF E'.
- **Why it's nonetheless true & the close-out plan:** carry an invariant `Good b E` :=
  *every coeff ≤ b+1, and any coeff-`(b+1)` term has tail `0`* (recursive on exponents & tails).
  Key facts to prove: (1) `Good (toONote b L)` [coeffs `< b`, trivial]; (2) `Good` preserved by
  `f b` [the new `b+1` coeff sits on a tail-`0` term — check the 6 `fundamentalSequence` `inr`
  forms] and by `pred`; (3) **`Good b E ∧ E successor ⟹ toONote(b+1)(evalNat b E') = E'`** —
  because in a `Good` successor, any `b+1` coeff is forced into the *finite lowest* term (else E
  is a limit, not a successor), which `pred` removes, leaving all coeffs `< b` ⇒ E' reconstructs.
  Then prove the general `hstep_pred_pow` for all NF E by WF recursion on `repr E` carrying `Good`,
  and `hstep_oadd_one_zero` is the `E = toONote b L` instance (NF via `toONote_NF`, `evalNat_toONote`).
