## 🧠 Context to carry forward

The mental model worth keeping is **why Fibonacci falls where Fermat and Mills resist**, because it
is the live crux for the phase-30 conjecture `DoubleExpTraceComposite`:

- The shared mechanism (phases 29/30) is Lagrange in `GL_n(𝔽_p)` with the modulus being the prime
  `p = t_n` itself: if `v_c|GL_n(𝔽_p)| ≤ n` then the sequence returns mod `p`, giving
  `p ∣ t_{n+j}` with `t_{n+j} > p` prime — contradiction. So `v_c|GL_n| > n` for all large `n`,
  which pins `t_n ≡ ±1` modulo a large power of `c`.
- For a **trace** sequence (Lucas, Fermat numbers, Mills primes) the Gauss congruence makes the
  terms *converge* `c`-adically, and the limit is allowed to sit at `±1`. That residual class is
  exactly the Fermat-hard leftover of phase 29.
- `F(2^n)` is a matrix **entry**, not a trace, and it *alternates*:
  `F(2^(n+1)) ≡ −F(2^n) (mod 2^(n+1))`. Adding the `±1` congruence at `n` and `n+1` then gives
  `s + s' ≡ 2h (mod 2^(n/2))` with `s, s' ∈ {±1}`; `h` even kills `s+s' = ±2` (they're `≢ 0 mod 4`),
  so `s + s' = 0` and `2^(n/2) ∣ 2h` for arbitrarily large `n`, forcing `h = 0`.
- Sanity check that the trick fails where it must: the Lucas analogue `L(2^n) + h` survives at
  exactly `h ∈ {0, 2}` (`L(2^n)+2 = L(2^(n−1))²` is composite; `L(2^n)` itself is the Fermat-like
  open case). That's the boundary, and it's the natural next formalization.

Two things I'd have wanted told to me: the **trace of `!![1,1;1,0]` is Lucas, not Fibonacci**, so
`SharedConjecture.exists_trace_pow_congr` cannot be applied directly — I cloned its proof entrywise
as the private `exists_entry_pow_congr` and read entry `(0,1)`. And only `2^(n+1) ∣ L(2^n)+1` is
needed, never the exact valuation, which keeps the induction to five lines.
