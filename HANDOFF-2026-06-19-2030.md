  keeps the argument fixed along the descent; Hardy's successor step shifts it (+1), so the
  Hardy transfer absorbs the +1 using monotonicity of intermediates — supplied by the WF IH

  value-domination and the reachability WF-recursion formulations; both stall identically at
  the diagonal — the limit case evaluates `f_{β[n]}` at argument `n` and bounding the
  fund-seq witness `i` (where `α < β[i]`) needs an explicit "norm" of `α` from the
  literature. The norm is the single missing ingredient. Do NOT re-derive that the diagonal
  blocks both formulations — it does. See `ON-LINE-REQUEST.md`.
- **B4 is a trap under mathlib's convention.** Measured: `H_{ω^0}=f_0`, `H_{ω^1}=f_1+1`, but
  `H_{ω^2}(2)=23 ≠ f_2(2)+1=9`. The textbook `H_{ω^α}=f_α` assumes `ω[n]=n`; mathlib uses
  `ω[n]=n+1`. No clean constant-shift identity — long-horizon, needs reformulation.
