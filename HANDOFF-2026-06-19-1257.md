## 🧠 Context to carry forward (the bootstrap payload)

rung is mechanical once you have them.

1. **The self-similarity TOWER** (`GoodsteinLike.lean`, axiom-clean). `GoodsteinLike a := ∀k,
   bump (base k)(a k) − 1 ≤ a(k+1)`. The genuine `goodsteinSeq m` satisfies it (equality). Two facts:
   `GoodsteinLike.dominates` (a dominates `goodsteinSeq (a 0)`) and `goodsteinLike_logSeq` (the leading
   exponent `logSeq a` of a Goodstein-like seq is itself Goodstein-like — the LEVEL-UP). Iterating gives
   the headline **`iterLeadExp_dominates m j`**: the `j`-fold iterated leading exponent of the seed-`m`
   descent dominates `goodsteinSeq ((log₂)^[j] m)`. j=1 = lap-9 self-similarity; each higher j = one
   ordinal level.

2. **The length BOOTSTRAP** (`two_mul_le_goodsteinLength_loglog` in `DominationOmega.lean`):
   `goodsteinLength ((log₂)^[2] m) ≥ 2m` (for `(log₂)^[2] m ≥ 2^16`). KEY MOVE: bootstrap `o=ω`
   *against itself*. The `f_ω` length bound is tower-strength: `goodsteinLength t ≥ f_ω(t)−2 =
   f_{t+1}(t)−2 ≥ f_3(t)−2 ≥ 2^{2^t·t}−2 ≥ 2(m+1)−2`. Bricks: `fastGrowing_omega_eq` (ω[t]=ofNat(t+1)),
   `fastGrowing_ofNat_mono` (index monotonicity, in `FastGrowing/Basic.lean`),
   `two_pow_le_fastGrowing_ofNat_three` (`f_3(t)=(f_2)^[t](t)`, f_2 expansive).

**The unifying ordinal bridge** `opow_le_seqONote_repr_of_toOrdinal`: `ω^β ≤ descent` ⟸
`β ≤ toOrdinal (base i)(leadExp_i)`. EVERY level is this lemma fed a `toOrdinal` lower bound on the
leading exponent (`opow_le_toOrdinal` for finite, `omega_omega_le_toOrdinal` for the large regime).

**The level pattern** (this is the mental model for the next rung):
- `o=ω`   needs leadExp ≥ base  (1st leadExp large regime). Length needed: trivial (m≥2^16).
- `o=ω^j` needs 2nd leadExp ≥ j (constant). Length needed: `goodsteinLength((log₂)^[2]m) ≥ m`.
- `o=ω^ω` needs 2nd leadExp ≥ base (large regime). Length needed: SAME `≥ 2m` bound — no new content!
- `o=ω^{ω^ω}` needs 3rd leadExp ≥ base → `goodsteinLength((log₂)^[3] m) ≥ 2m`.

**Why the next rung is genuinely hard (don't waste a lap on the easy-looking route):** the deeper
length bound `goodsteinLength((log₂)^[3] m) ≥ 2m` does NOT follow by reapplying
`two_mul_le_goodsteinLength_loglog` (that gives only `≥ 2·log₂ m`, checked — too weak). It needs an
`f_{ω^ω}`-strength lower bound at the deep seed, i.e. a fastGrowing lower bound `f_{ω^ω}(t) ≥ triple-exp`.
**Fast-growing lower bounds at LIMIT ordinals are the irreducible hard recursive piece** — that IS the
"`f_{ε₀}`" content. `f_{ω^2}` machinery already exists in `FastGrowing/Basic.lean`
(`fastGrowing_omega_sq_index_step`, `fastGrowing_monotone_omega_sq`) — start there.
