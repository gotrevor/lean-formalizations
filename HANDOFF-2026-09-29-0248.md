## 🧠 Context to carry forward
Stephan's `Prop` is `{β : ℚ | |ξ − β| · ∏_{l∈S₁}|β.num|_l · ∏_{l∈S₂}|β.den|_l ≤ H(β)^(−2−ε)}` is
finite, with `H(β) = max(|β.num|, β.den)`.  Two things about it shaped the whole file:

1. **It is phrased on `β`'s lowest-terms data.**  `Edges.lean`'s `mahler_mul_of_ridout1957` gets to
   pick unreduced `(p, q) = (P vⁿ, uⁿ)` and injects `n ↦ uⁿ`.  Here reduction by
   `g = gcd(P vⁿ, uⁿ)` destroys the `n`-marker, so **the injectivity route is not available**.
   I replaced it with a *positive-gap* argument: a finite set of rationals all `≠ q` keeps a
   positive distance `m` from `q` (`Finset.min'` over the image of the `erase`, case-split on
   empty), while `|q − β| ≤ (v/u)ⁿ → 0`.  That is shorter and more robust; prefer it in future.
2. **Everything about `β = P vⁿ / uⁿ` follows from one identity**, the cross-multiplication
   `β.num · uⁿ = P vⁿ · β.den` (from `Rat.num_div_den` + `div_eq_div_iff`).  From it:
   `vⁿ ∣ β.num` (coprime to `uⁿ`), `β.den ∣ uⁿ`, and `β.num ≤ 2q β.den` (using `P ≤ 2q αⁿ`).
   Don't go near `Rat.den_dvd`/`divInt`; the identity is strictly easier.

The arithmetic then closes with `ε' = ε/(2 log u)`: product `≤ e^(−εn)/(uⁿ β.den)`,
`H(β) ≤ 2q β.den`, and after `Real.rpow_le_rpow_of_nonpos` the target reduces to
`e^(−εn)·(2q)^(2+ε')·β.den^(1+ε') ≤ uⁿ`; `β.den ≤ uⁿ` gives `β.den^(ε') ≤ e^(nε/2)`, leaving the
pure constant condition `(2q)^(2+ε') ≤ e^(nε/2)` — true past an explicit `n₀`.  **Only
`β.den ≤ uⁿ` is used about the hidden gcd**, which is why the bookkeeping stays short.

Abandoned: computing `β.num`/`β.den` explicitly as `P vⁿ/g`, `uⁿ/g`.  Never needed.
