## 🧠 Context to carry forward

**The crux, and it was a real one.**  Phase 34 lifted `A^(c+1) ≡ −I (mod c)` up the tower and then
*divided by* `A^(c^n)` using an explicit integer inverse `fibMinv` — which exists only because
`det(fibM) = −1` is a unit.  For general `Q`, `det(A) = Q` is **not** a unit, so that step has no
analogue, and it was the single blocker whose failure would have forced a redesign.  The fix is the
**adjugate**: `A^N · adj(A^N) = det(A^N) • 1 = Q^N • 1`.  Multiply the frobenius congruence on the
right by `adj(A^N)`, read entry `(1,0)` (which is `−(A^N) 1 0 = −U(c^n)` by
`Matrix.adjugate_fin_two`, so no entry formula for `A^N` is needed at all), and cancel `Q^N` by
`IsCoprime (c^(n+1)) (Q^N)`.  Generalizable lesson: **the adjugate needs the determinant only
coprime to the modulus, never a unit** — strictly weaker than invertibility and exactly what a
`p`-adic lift has available.  Flushed to auto-memory as
`adjugate-not-inverse-for-matrix-congruence`.

**Frobenius is now `≡ Q·I`, not `≡ −I`.**  In `K = (ZMod c)[X]/(X² − PX + Q)` the root `x` has
`x^c = P − x` (the conjugate), so `x^(c+1) = x(P − x) = Q`.  Reading off the basis `(x, 1)` via
`x^(N+1) = U(N+1)·x − Q·U(N)` (`pow_eq_lucasU`) gives `c ∣ U(c+1)` and `c ∣ Q(U(c)+1)`; then
`c ∤ Q` gives `c ∣ U(c) + 1`.

**`hD` is doing more work than it looks.**  `¬ IsSquare ((P²−4Q : ℤ) : ZMod c)` *by itself* forces
`c ≠ 2` (every element of `ZMod 2` is a square), `c ∤ Q` and `Q ≠ 0` (else `D ≡ P²`).  That is why
frozen statement 1 legitimately carries no `hQ`/`hc2` hypothesis — don't "fix" it by adding them.

**Approach not taken (and why it's fine).**  I considered identifying `A` with multiplication-by-`x`
on `K` to get `A^(c+1) ≡ Q·I` structurally.  It is elegant but formalizing "matrix of a
multiplication map" costs more than the two entry lemmas
(`lucasA_pow_apply_zero_zero`, `lucasA_pow_succ_apply_one`) that give all four entries of `A^(c+1)`
directly from `c ∣ U(c+1)` and `c ∣ U(c)+1`.  Don't reopen it.

**Everything in phase 33's `LucasTwoPow` is `private`.**  Its `lucasMat`, `uSeq`, `vLuc`,
`lucasMat_pow` are unusable downstream; `LucasInert` therefore has its own public `lucasA` and entry
lemmas.  Any future Lucas phase should reuse `LucasInert`'s public copies, not re-derive them again.
