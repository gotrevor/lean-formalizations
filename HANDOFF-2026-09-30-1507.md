## 🧠 Context to carry forward

The one thing worth carrying: **the route written in the file header does not work, and the file
header was NOT edited** (frozen). Read this before trusting it.

The header proposes building `σ : q(B̄) ↦ q(B̄^c)` on `R[B̄]`, proving "σ-fixed ring = scalars", then
pinning the constant by taking traces. The trace step gives only `d·s ≡ d·tr B (mod c^(n+1))`, so
**it cannot pin `s` when `c ∣ d`** — the header flags this and offers two patches (identify `S`
with the algebra trace of `R[B̄]`; or induct on `S − tr(B̄)·I`). Both were considered and rejected:
the first needs `Tr_{R[x]}(x^m) = tr(B^m)`, i.e. Newton's identities, which mathlib does not hand
you conveniently; the second hits the same divide-by-`d` wall at every level of the induction.

What replaced it (and why it's better): make the *roots* the primitive object rather than `σ`.

1. `χ_B(B^(c^k)) ≡ 0 (mod c^(n+1))` for **every** `k` — phase 50's `aeval_charpoly_prime_pow_congr`
   at level `n+k−1`, plus `charpoly_coeff_tower_congr` (charpolys are constant along the tower mod
   `c^(n+1)`). Note this is needed at all `k`, not just `k = 1`; the header's `σ` route only ever
   had the `k = 1` input.
2. A transfer lemma `coeff_dvd_of_aeval_dvd` converts that *matrix* congruence into a *polynomial*
   divisibility `χ̄_B ∣ χ̄_B(X^(c^k))` over `ZMod (c^(n+1))`. This is the step the header hid inside
   "σ is well defined". Proof is an induction on the precision `m`; the `m = 1` base is
   `eq_zero_of_aeval_mapMatrix_eq_zero` (χ̄_B irreducible ⇒ it *is* the minimal polynomial of `B̄`,
   by Bézout in `(ZMod c)[X]`).
3. So in `T = AdjoinRoot χ̄_B` the `d` elements `x^(c^k)` are roots. Their pairwise differences are
   **units**: `T` is local (kernel of `T → AdjoinRoot χ̄_A` is `cT`, and `(c:T)^(n+1) = 0`), and in
   the residue field `𝔽_(c^d)` the Frobenius orbit of the generator is free — otherwise
   `y^(c^m) = y` with `0 < m < d` and all `c^d` elements are roots of `X^(c^m) − X`.
4. A monic degree-`d` polynomial over *any* `CommRing` with `d` roots of pairwise unit difference
   equals `∏ (X − C r_k)`. Then `prod_X_sub_C_nextCoeff` gives `Σ x^(c^k) = tr B` **exactly** —
   `c ∣ d` or not. This is the whole point: unit differences buy what "distinct" cannot.
5. Evaluate the `R[X]` identity at `B̄` with `aeval` (Cayley–Hamilton kills `χ̄_B`). No ring hom into
   the matrix ring is needed — `AdjoinRoot.lift` requires a commutative target and would have been
   a dead end.

Statement 1 (the period) is independent of all that and much cheaper.
