## 🧠 Context to carry forward (read this — it's the mental model)
The axiom is the **cross-scale orchestration**: from an arbitrary Hausdorff cover, extract a dominant
dyadic scale `j` and a `2⁻ʲ`-separated net of `≈2ʲ` directions whose segments are covered `≳1/poly(j)`
at scale `j`, so the localized Córdoba count forces `≳2^{j(2-d)}/poly` pieces ⟹ `∑ediam^d ≳ 1`.

The **net-scale circularity** (the historic blocker): Córdoba needs the net `2⁻ʲ`-separated with the
SAME `j` the pigeonhole outputs. **Resolution = the shift average** (NEW this lap, fully proven): run
a FINE `2⁻ᴶ`-net (finitely many directions ⟹ finitely many base points ⟹ NO measurability issue);
pigeonhole to a global dominant scale `j*`; then decompose the fine range into the `2^{J-j*}` dyadic
shifts of the `2⁻ʲ*`-subnet — **some shift retains ≥ the full average** of the fine net's
dominant-scale covered length, so thinning fine→coarse loses nothing. The subnet has base angle
`c = β·2⁻ᴶ` (hence the base-angle Córdoba generalization, R2). The earlier "diverge to 0" dead ends
(naive Cauchy–Schwarz over scales, full-net LP) were artifacts of the UNSHIFTED/fixed net — don't
relitigate them.

**Two subtleties I worked out:**
- *Union vs sum*: the covered set's measure is `vol(⋃ Tₙ)`, NOT `∑ vol(Tₙ)` (same-scale pieces overlap;
  the sum overcounts). So the dyadic pigeonhole runs on union measures (`one_le_tsum_volume_fiber_union`).
- *Measurability*: covered sets must be measurable, but a general Kakeya cover piece needn't be. Fix:
  replace each `tₙ` by `closure tₙ` (same `ediam`, still covers `S`) ⟹ `φ⁻¹(closed)` measurable
  (`exists_measurable_pullback_cover`). The content bound is unaffected since `ediam(closure)=ediam`.

**⚠️ FAITHFULNESS FINDING:** the cited axiom uses the **unshifted** grid `dir(k·2⁻ʲ)`. The shift
average proves only the **shifted** form `∃ c, dir(c+k·2⁻ʲ)`; the unshifted (`c=0`) may be
adversarially defeatable. So the axiom is SUFFICIENT for the theorem (valid kernel proof) but its OWN
truth is uncertain. **Plan: discard the unshifted axiom and prove `kakeya_hausdorffContentBound`
DIRECTLY** (the consumer) in the shifted form — that's both the faithfulness fix and what the proven
bricks discharge. (Don't try to prove the unshifted axiom; it may be false.)
