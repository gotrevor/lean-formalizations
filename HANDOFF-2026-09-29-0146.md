The decisive structural insight, and the reason the lap went fast: **all four of Bala's "general"
divisibilities are corollaries of one lemma nobody had stated.** Rather than proving
`sub_dvd_sub_lin` / `sub_dvd_sub_mul` / `sub_dvd_det` separately, I introduced

```lean
theorem sub_dvd_sub_addMul (n k : ℕ) : ∀ d B : ℕ, u n - u k ∣ u (B + d * n) - u (B + d * k)
```

by induction on `d`, splitting `u(B+dn+n) − u(B+dk+k)` into two pieces that are each a
`sub_dvd_sub_shift` (already in `Sylvester.lean`) — one of the IH, one of `u n − u k` itself.
Then `sub_dvd_sub_lin` is an `r = s+d` / `s = r+d` case-split (the second branch is `.neg_right` of
the first), `sub_dvd_sub_mul` is `B = 0`, and `sub_dvd_det` is a two-term telescope
`u(sk)(u(rn)−u(rk)) − u(rk)(u(sn)−u(sk))`. If a future lap adds more Bala index identities, reach
for `sub_dvd_sub_addMul` first.

Two other routes worth knowing, both chosen over the obvious alternative:

* **`HasSum (1/a i) 1`** — I deliberately did *not* prove summability by comparison with a geometric
  series. Establishing `2^n ≤ a n` is fiddly at small `n` (needs `a n ≥ 3` for `n ≥ 1` as a side
  condition). Instead: terms are nonneg and the closed form `Σ_{i<n} = 1 − 1/(a n − 1) ≤ 1` bounds
  the partial sums, so `summable_of_sum_range_le` gives summability directly; then
  `tendsto_nhds_unique … .tendsto_sum_nat` against `1 − 1/(a n − 1) → 1`, the limit coming from
  `squeeze_zero` versus `1/(n+1)` using `a n ≥ n + 2`. This needs the closed form over **ℝ as well
  as ℚ** — `sylvesterSeq_sum_inv_real` is a duplicated induction on purpose (casting the ℚ identity
  was more friction than re-running four lines).
* **`−3` a QR at every prime factor** is an *identity*, not a computation:
  `4·a(n+1) = (2 a n − 1)² + 3`, so `(2 a n − 1)²  = −3` in `ZMod p` the moment `p ∣ a(n+1)`. The
  `n = 0` leaf is separate (`p ∣ 2` ⟹ `p = 2`).

The one step with genuine arithmetic content is **mod 864**: the induction needs
`864 ∣ 432·n·(1+3n)`, which holds because exactly one of `n`, `1+3n` is even. That parity *is* why
the progression has 24 terms and not 48 — worth remembering if anyone questions the modulus.

Also discovered: **Mohanty's `0 < m` hypothesis is unnecessary.** Pairwise coprimality follows from
`IsCoprime m (b n)` for all `n` (propagated by `IsCoprime.add_mul_left_right`, since
`b(n+1) = b n² + m(1 − b n)`) plus `b i ∣ b j − m` for `i < j`, transported by the existing
`intGcd_congr_of_dvd_sub`. I kept `hm` in the signature because the phase-11 statements are frozen
by name; it produces an unused-variable warning, which is expected and harmless.
