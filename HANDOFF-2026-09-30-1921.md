## 🧠 Context to carry forward
The interesting structural point, and the reason this phase was worth planting: phase 45 proved the
analogous statement for `ξ(3^k − 2)` but had to lean on our own **open** node
`ShiftedTraceRigidity`. Phase 58 needs **no such node** — the whole cubic-Pisot branch is killed
outright, because phase 57 gave Theorem D in full and the shift `s ≥ 8` makes its size condition
(`α^s > d + 1 = 4`) reachable from Siegel's lower bound on Pisot numbers alone.

Mental model of the proof in four moves:
1. **Saito's hypotheses.** The subtle one is `(B5′)`: it demands a *single* `k` carrying both
   `C_m ∣ C_k` **and** the `29/10` ratio. Euler gives divisibility at `k = m + t` for *any* `t`
   that is a multiple of `φ(C_m)`, so `t` is free to be huge — take `t = φ(C_m)·i` with
   `i := 19s + m + 1`, and `3^t > t ≥ i` drags `k` past the ratio threshold `19s ≤ 3^(k+j)`.
   That "multiples of φ are all available, so spend them on a second constraint" move is the same
   trick phase 45 used, generalized.
2. **`g = 1`.** `g ∣ 3C_k − C_(k+1) = 2s`; `s` even ⇒ `C_k` odd ⇒ `g` odd ⇒ `g ∣ s`; then
   `g ∣ 3^(k+j)` while `3 ∤ g` (because `3 ∤ s`). So `hs_even` and `hs3` are load-bearing exactly
   here, and `hj` is load-bearing exactly in the doubling bound.
3. **Branch `f ≢ X³ mod 3`:** phase 57's `floor_pow_prime_pow_add_not_prime_full` at `c = 3`
   produces infinitely many non-prime floors; pick one with `n ≥ j+1` and it collides with
   `hξ.1.2 (n − j)`.
4. **Branch `f ≡ X³ mod 3`:** the companion matrix is nilpotent mod 3 (`aeval_compM_self` +
   `hX`), so `3 ∣ traceSeq f N` for every `N ≥ 3`. Saito's Prop 3.1(iv) makes that trace **exactly**
   the prime floor, forcing the prime to be 3, while `exists_floor_gt` says the floor exceeds 3.

**Approach *not* taken, deliberately:** in branch 4 do **not** reach for
`eventually_floor_eq_traceSeq` — it only gives `⌊α^N⌋ ∈ {V_N, V_N − 1}`, and the `−1` destroys the
divisibility argument. The exact equality has to come from Saito's trace identity. That is why
`powTrace_eq_traceSeq` had to be built.
