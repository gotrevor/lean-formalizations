## The route for the crux (the only non-routine part)

Suppose all four `e^{xᵢyⱼ}` are algebraic; write `λᵢⱼ = xᵢyⱼ`, so `λ₀₀λ₁₁ = λ₀₁λ₁₀`.

1. `algebraicIndependent_of_exp_isAlgebraic` — **Schanuel ⇒ ℚ-linearly independent logarithms of
   algebraic numbers are algebraically independent.**  Apply `algebraicIndependent_of_schanuel`
   with `y = z`: the exponentials are algebraic over `ℚ`, hence over `ℚ(z)`, so the Schanuel
   field is algebraic over `ℚ(z)` and the counting step fires.  This is the *only* use of
   Schanuel in the whole proof.
2. `false_of_algebraicIndependent_mem` — **the rank obstruction.**  If `y : Fin n → ℂ` is
   algebraically independent and every `y i` lies in `ℚ(range w)` for a *non-injective*
   `w : Fin n → ℂ`, that is `False`: `trdeg ℚ(y) ≥ n` transports along
   `trdeg_le_adjoin_of_forall_isAlgebraic` to `trdeg ℚ(w) ≥ n`, and
   `algebraicIndependent_of_le_trdeg_adjoin` would then make `w` injective.
3. Four cases, by how far `dim_ℚ span{λᵢⱼ}` drops below 4 (extracted with
   `linearIndependent_finSnoc` + `Submodule.mem_span_range_iff_exists_fun`, via the little
   `snoc3`/`snoc4` bridges).  In each case one member of a maximal ℚ-independent subfamily is
   solved *by a single division* out of the relation and lands in the field generated by the
   others:
   * all four independent: `λ₁₁ = λ₀₁λ₁₀/λ₀₀ ∈ ℚ(λ₀₀,λ₀₁,λ₁₀)`;
   * `λ₀₀,λ₀₁,λ₁₀` independent, `λ₁₁ = p λ₀₀ + q λ₀₁ + r λ₁₀`: then
     `λ₁₀ = −(p λ₀₀² + q λ₀₀λ₀₁)/(r λ₀₀ − λ₀₁) ∈ ℚ(λ₀₀,λ₀₁)` (denominator nonzero by the
     independence of `λ₀₀, λ₀₁`);
   * `λ₁₀ = p λ₀₀ + q λ₀₁` but `λ₀₀,λ₀₁,λ₁₁` independent: `λ₁₁ ∈ ℚ(λ₀₀,λ₀₁)` likewise;
   * everything in `span{λ₀₀,λ₀₁}` (`dim = 2`): the relation makes `λ₀₁` a root of a **monic**
     quadratic over `ℚ(λ₀₀)`, so `Monic.ne_zero` gives a nonzero polynomial for free and
     `not_isAlgebraic_of_algebraicIndependent_pair` closes it.  `q ≠ 0` there because `q = 0`
     would give `λ₁₀ = p λ₀₀`, against `LinearIndependent ℚ ![λ₀₀, λ₁₀]`.

The point worth reusing: **no MvPolynomial coefficient extraction anywhere.**  Packaging the
degenerate cases as "linear independence of a *pair* of the `λ`s" (which comes straight from the
independence of `x` and of `y`, via `linearIndependent_const_mul`) is what removes them.

## Gotchas hit

* A multi-line `by ... using` term inside parentheses does not parse when the continuation lines
  are indented under the enclosing `refine`.  Use `show _ ∈ L; rw [h]; exact …` instead of
  `simpa [h] using …` when the term spans lines.
* `fin_cases i` leaves indices as `⟨0, ⋯⟩`, so `Matrix.cons_val_zero` alone does not fire; add
  `Fin.zero_eta, Fin.mk_one, Fin.isValue`.
* `LinearIndependent.map'` produces the family in `⇑f ∘ v` form — `simp only [Function.comp_def]`
  before `rw`ing a `funext`-proved equality of the `fun i => …` form.
* `algebraMap ℚ ℂ q` vs `(q : ℂ)`: `eq_ratCast` as a `simp only` lemma, then `linear_combination`.
