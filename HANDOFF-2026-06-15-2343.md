## ⚠️ Gotchas
- **`linear_combination` won't unfold a `def` to get the `Eq`.** `h : OnLine A B p`
  (a def) → `linear_combination -h` silently contributes nothing (residual = whole goal).
  Fix: `simp only [OnLine] at h` first. (But passing such `h` directly as a lemma arg
  works — unification unfolds reducible defs; `linear_combination` doesn't.)
- **`inter_cc` etc. need incidence, not uniqueness** — a point is constructible if it lies
  on both circles; you needn't prove it's *the* intersection. This is what makes
  `cp_translate` a one-liner (`ring` on both incidence eqns).
- **Term-mode arg mis-ordering**: `exact f (by linear_combination …) ecirc` can leave x,y
  as metavars when the tactic-block arg precedes the arg that pins them. Use tactic mode +
  a `have` for the equation (see `circle_meet_circle`).
- **Namespace shadowing**: defining `IsConstructible.sq` broke the existing `rw [sq]`
  inside the `IsConstructible` namespace (resolved to the new lemma). Use `pow_two`/the
  `.sq` lemma instead of `rw [sq]`.
- Pre-existing harmless lint warnings in `Curtis/Lemma2.lean` — leave them.
