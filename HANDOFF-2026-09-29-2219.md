## 🧠 Context to carry forward

The lap's one real insight, worth keeping if this line is ever reopened:

- **Do the whole Lucas toolkit through the companion matrix, not through identities.**
  With `A = !![P,-Q;1,0]` and two scalar recursions `u` (`0,1`) and `v` (`1,0`),
  `A^N = !![u(N+1), v(N+1); u N, v N]`.  Then `V m := u(m+1) + v m` *is* `tr(A^m)`, and both
  doubling identities fall out of `A^(2m) = (A^m)^2` alone: entry `(1,0)` gives
  `U(2m) = U(m)V(m)`, and `tr(M²) = tr(M)² − 2 det M` with `det(A^m) = Q^m` gives
  `V(2m) = V(m)² − 2Q^m`.  Phase 32 proved the Fibonacci versions via `Nat.fib_two_mul`,
  `Nat.fib_two_mul_add_one` and a hand-rolled Cassini identity; the matrix route is strictly
  shorter and needs **no** addition formula and **no** Cassini.  It also generalises `v` for free
  (`v N = u(N+1) − P u N`, proved by a two-step induction carried as a conjunction).

- **The structural difference from phase 32 is the loss of monotonicity.**  `U(2^n)` need not be
  positive or increasing; the hypothesis is only `|U(2^n)| → ∞`.  Phase 32's endgame
  (`p_n ∣ p_{n+j}`, both prime, `p_{n+j} > p_n` ⟹ absurd) therefore dies.  The replacement, which
  is the genuinely new bit of this lap: the `GL₂` mechanism only yields `|t_{n+j}| = |t_n|`, but
  the hypothesis it consumes (`v₂(glCard 2 p) ≤ m`) **survives the step** — the modulus `p` is
  unchanged and the index only grows — so you can *iterate* it and manufacture `|t_{n'}| = |t_n|`
  for arbitrarily large `n'`, contradicting `|t_n| → ∞`.  If a future phase drops monotonicity
  again, reach for this iteration pattern first.

- Oddness of `U(2^n)` is a **joint** induction with oddness of `V(2^n)` (`V(2m) = V(m)²−2Q^m`);
  phase 32's `Nat.fib_gcd` parity trick has no Lucas analogue.

- Nothing was abandoned this lap — the header's route worked as written, first attempt, including
  the `2^(n+1) ∣ Q^(2^n) − 1` term the header predicted would be the extra work in the sign flip.
