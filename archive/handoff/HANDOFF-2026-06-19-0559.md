## 🧠 Context to carry forward (the mental model)
- **A4's real content was the CNF "norm" budget condition.** The classical literature ask
  (`ON-LINE-REQUEST`, now removed) was self-resolved: define `norm α` = the max finite
  number anywhere in `α`'s CNF. The key theorem `lt_fundamentalSequence_of_norm_le` says:
  for a limit `β` and `α < β` with `norm α ≤ x`, already `α < g_β(x)`. **Why it works
  inductively:** `reaches_of_lt` descends `β` toward a *fixed* `α`, so the budget condition
  `norm α ≤ x` is preserved across the whole descent — that's the crux that made the
  recursion close. Proved by structural induction over all 6 `fundamentalSequence` branches
  (the 5 limit branches use either the IH on exponent/tail or the single-level helper
  `lt_oadd_of_lead_le`). Don't re-derive — it's done.
- **Strictness needed `osucc`.** `Reaches` value-transfer only gives `≤` (limit steps are
  equalities). To get strict `<` we route through `osucc o` (notation successor, structural
  def so `fundamentalSequence (osucc o) = inl (some o)` is transparent), reach it, then take
  one strict successor index step (`fastGrowing_lt_succ_index`, needs `2 ≤ n`). This is why
  the core lemma now requires `2 ≤ n` and `norm o < n` (was wrongly stated with just `1 ≤ n`
  — it's **false at n=1**: `f_0(1)=2=f_1(1)`).
- **ONote is only a `Preorder`, NOT a `LinearOrder`** (repr isn't injective off NF). So
  `lt_trichotomy`/`not_lt` fail on `ONote`. Do trichotomy at the `repr` (Ordinal) level and
  convert with `lt_def`/`repr_inj`; use `lt_asymm` not `not_lt`. (`lt_oadd_cases` does this.)
- **C2 bridge:** `Engine.toOrdinal b n` = CNF of `n` in base `b` with `b↦ω` = exactly
  `ONote.repr` of the corresponding notation. `toONote b n` mirrors `toOrdinal`'s recursion;
  `repr_toONote` (= toOrdinal) and `toONote_NF` are proved (NF's leading-exponent obligation
  reuses the public `toOrdinal_mono_and_bound` remainder bound). Then `seqONote m k :=
  toONote (k+2) (goodsteinSeq m k)`, and `seqONote_lt` transports `Engine.seqOrd_step` to a
  strict `ONote` descent. `seqONote_eq_zero_iff` + `seqONote_goodsteinLength` +
  `seqONote_ne_zero_of_lt` pin `goodsteinLength m` as *exactly* the descent's first-zero index.
