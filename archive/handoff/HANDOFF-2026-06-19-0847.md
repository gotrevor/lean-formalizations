Headline target (DIRECTION.md C3): "**`goodsteinLength` eventually dominates every `fastGrowing o`**".
We have `goodsteinLength m = H_{toONote 2 m}(2) − 2` — a Hardy value with large *index* but
**argument fixed at 2**. I attempted to dominate it by `hardy_le_of_lt` (Hardy index monotonicity)
comparing against `ω^o + (m+2)` at arg 2, and it **does not work**:
`hardy_le_of_lt` requires a budget hypothesis `norm α ≤ x`, and index-monotonicity GENUINELY FAILS
at small fixed argument — **measured: `H_ω(2)=5 < H_5(2)=7` although `ω > 5`**. A big-coefficient
notation like `ω^o+(m+2)` has `norm = m+2 > 2`, so the comparison is invalid. The naive arg-2
approach is mathematically wrong, not just hard. The headline needs a **budget-aware** argument:
the descent itself supplies a growing budget, and `hardy_le_of_lt` only unlocks once `norm ≤ arg`.
