## 🧠 Context to carry forward (the mental model — READ THIS)
The crux is a **growth lower bound**. The technique that works (and the one that DOESN'T):

- **What works — per-step rate bounds + plateau, one level at a time:**
  - `bump b n ≥ n+1` for `n ≥ b` (`bump_gt`): one bump strictly grows a value above its base.
  - The value drops by `≤1`/step (`bump ≥ id`) AND grows while `≥ base` ⟹ it stays `≥ m` for the
    first `m` steps (`goodsteinSeq_ge_init`) ⟹ `seqOrd ≥ ω` for `~m` steps = **sub-fact (ii) at o=1**.
  - One level up: the **leading exponent** `L_k = log_{base k}(G_k)` *bumps itself*
    (`log_bump`: `log_{b+1}(bump b n) = bump b (log_b n)`), drops by `≤1`/step
    (`leadExp_drop_le_one`), is non-decreasing while `≥ base` (`leadExp_ge_of_base_le`). Telescoped
    (`leadExp_ge_sub`: `L_i ≥ log₂ m − i`) + ordinal bridge (`opow_le_seqONote_repr`) ⟹ descent
    `≥ ω^k` for `~log₂ m − k` steps.

- **The KEY tension (why the headline isn't closed):** budget and leadExp **trade off**. The
  non-diagonal reduction (`fastGrowing_step_le_goodsteinLength`) at step `j` gives `f_o(j+2) ≤
  goodsteinLength m + 2`. To have `leadExp ≥ n` you must take `j ≈ log₂ m − n` (small), so the budget
  is `~log₂ m`, NOT `m`. This proves goodsteinLength is **non-elementary** (`f_n(log₂ m − n + 2) ≤
  goodsteinLength m + 2`, tower of height `~log₂ m` at `n ≈ log₂ m/2`) but NOT the diagonal `f_n(m)`.

- **Why the diagonal needs more (the remaining DEEP crux):** the leading exponent stays `≥ n` for FAR
  more than `log₂ m` steps in reality — drops are RARE (only at pure-power boundaries). The number of
  steps between consecutive drops of `L_k` from level `E` to `E−1` is **itself a Goodstein length of
  the sub-structure at level `E`** (astronomically `≫ m`). The per-step `drop ≤1` is just a rate
  bound; the actual rare-drop behaviour is the genuine Cichoń recursion. Formalizing
  "steps-between-drops = sub-Goodstein-length" (likely induction on the leading exponent mirroring
  `hardy_oadd_iter`) is the multi-lap obligation that closes sub-fact (ii) and the headline.

- **Dead ends already mapped (don't retry):** (a) fixed-argument-2 Hardy index-monotonicity FAILS
