  Each vanishing substitution makes `linForm p k=(k-2)X₂+X₃-p` a root; the (p-1)/2
  forms are distinct (X₂-coeff `(k:ℂ)-2`), so `card_roots'` gives the bound. **Done, axiom-clean.**

  is fiddly: extracting `∑ i, m i = d` from `IsHomogeneous` needs the `Finsupp.weight`/

  `C a ↦ a` cleanly (used in the `linForm` injectivity proof).

  had to inline the type. Avoid `set` on types used inside simp targets.
