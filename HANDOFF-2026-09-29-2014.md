## 🧠 Context to carry forward

**The one idea that made phase 30 cheap.** Phase 29's step 1 concluded `p ∣ tr C^(3^(m+j))`. That
shape does not generalise to the shifted sequence `tr C^(c^k) + h`. Restating it as a *congruence*

`exists_trace_pow_congr : ∃ j ≥ 1, (p:ℤ) ∣ tr C^(c^(m+j)) − tr C^(c^m)`

makes `+ h` cancel identically, so the shifted case needs no second proof, and the base generalises
`3 → c` by textual substitution (primality of `c` is used only in
`Nat.Prime.coprime_iff_not_dvd hc` and `hc.pos`). Carry this: whenever a phase-29-style periodicity
result must be reused at a shift, state it as a congruence between two terms, not a divisibility of
one.

**The ThreeAdic refactor.** `mills_threeAdic`'s body was ~78 lines of glue (Saito dichotomy → Vieta
→ `companion3` → floor = trace → prime + monotone). That is now the public reusable
`ThreeAdic.exists_companion_of_algebraic_mills`; `mills_threeAdic` is the `obtain` plus the step-3
assembly. Frozen statements were not touched; verified by re-running `#print axioms` on all five.

**Fermat is entirely trivial given the conjecture** — `C = Matrix.diagonal ![2,1]`, `c = 2`,
`Matrix.diagonal_pow` gives `tr C^N = 2^N + 1`. Growth is `k < 2^k ≤ 2^(2^k)`
(`Nat.lt_two_pow_self` twice). No Fermat-specific number theory enters, which is the point of the
write-up: the conjecture is genuinely the *shared* content.

**Assumption to be honest about.** `DoubleExpTraceComposite` as stated is presumably open; the
growth hypothesis is necessary (eigenvalues `λ, −λ` with odd `c` give trace `0`). Nothing in this
lap makes it plausible beyond the `1/log X` heuristic already in the file header.
