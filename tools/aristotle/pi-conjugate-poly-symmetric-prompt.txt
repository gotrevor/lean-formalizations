open Polynomial

/-- If theta : Fin n -> C are exactly the complex roots (with multiplicity) of a monic
G : Q[X], then the product over all subsets of (X - C (sum of theta over the subset)) has
every coefficient in the range of (algebraMap Q C). I.e. the subset-sum polynomial descends
to Q[X]. -/
theorem subsetSum_poly_descends (n : Nat) (theta : Fin n -> Complex) (G : Polynomial Rat)
    (hGmonic : G.Monic)
    (hroots : (G.map (algebraMap Rat Complex)).roots
      = (Finset.univ : Finset (Fin n)).val.map theta) :
    forall j : Nat,
      (Finset.prod (Finset.univ.powerset)
        (fun t => Polynomial.X - Polynomial.C (Finset.sum t (fun k => theta k)))).coeff j
        in Set.range (algebraMap Rat Complex) := by
  sorry

Mathematical strategy: each coefficient of the product is, up to sign, an elementary
symmetric function of the family { sum_{k in t} theta k : t subset of Fin n }. Permuting the
theta_k permutes this family, so the coefficient is a SYMMETRIC polynomial (with integer
coefficients) in theta_1,...,theta_n. By the fundamental theorem of symmetric polynomials
(MvPolynomial.esymmAlgEquiv / esymmAlgHom) it is a polynomial in the elementary symmetric
functions e_j(theta); and e_j(theta) = (up to sign) the coefficients of G (Vieta:
Polynomial.coeff_eq_esymm_roots_of_card / Multiset.prod_X_sub_C_coeff), which lie in Q.
Hence each coefficient is in the range of algebraMap Q C. Relevant mathlib:
MvPolynomial.IsSymmetric, MvPolynomial.esymm, Multiset.esymm, Polynomial.coeff,
Vieta lemmas in RingTheory/Polynomial/Vieta.lean.
