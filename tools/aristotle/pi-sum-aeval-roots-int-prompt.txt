Mathematical strategy (any correct route is fine):
The sum equals sum over k of g.coeff k times the k-th power sum p_k of the roots.
Each power sum p_k of the roots of a monic integer polynomial is an integer, because
power sums are integer polynomials in the elementary symmetric functions of the roots
(Newton's identities), and the elementary symmetric functions of the roots of a monic
integer polynomial are (up to sign) its coefficients, hence integers.
An alternative clean route: let C be the companion matrix of f over Int (a d-by-d
integer matrix whose characteristic polynomial is f and whose complex eigenvalues, with
multiplicity, are exactly f.aroots Complex). Then sum over roots r of g(r) equals the
trace of the integer matrix g(C) (Matrix.trace of an integer matrix is an integer, and
trace equals the sum of eigenvalues over an algebraically closed field). Relevant mathlib:
Matrix.charpoly, the companion matrix, Matrix.trace_eq_sum_roots_charpoly or eigenvalue
APIs, Polynomial.aeval over matrices.
