## 🧠 Context to carry forward

**The mental model of Step 3.** Two independent squeezes on the same prime `p = |tr C^(3^n−2)|`:

1. *From above, via growth.* If `v₃(|GL₃(𝔽_p)|) ≤ n`, the order of `C` mod `p` divides
   `3^n(3^(kj) − 1)` for a fixed period `j` and every `k`. That exponent is exactly the gap
   `(3^(n+kj) − 2) − (3^n − 2)`, so `p` divides a *later* trace. Since `p` already divides its
   own trace, `p` divides that later trace too; the later trace is prime and (by growth) bigger
   in absolute value than `p` — so `p` equals it and is smaller than it. Contradiction. Hence
   `n < v₃(|GL₃(𝔽_p)|)`.
2. *From below, via the group order.* Phase 44's
   `exists_pow_sub_one_of_lt_padicValNat_glCard` then hands back `i ∈ {1,2,3}` with
   `3^(n/3) ∣ p^i − 1`, and elementary arithmetic turns that into `p ≡ ±1 (mod 3^(n/3 − 1))`.

**The one genuine improvement over the planted route** (worth keeping in your head, because it
generalises): the header route handles `i = 3` by case-splitting on `p mod 3`. Unnecessary —
`y² + y + 1 = 0` has **no root in `ZMod 9` whatsoever**, so `v₃(p² + p + 1) ≤ 1` for *every* `p`,
hypothesis-free. That single fact is the entire reason the window is `3^(n/3 − 1)` and not
`3^(n/3)`, and it let the `3 ∤ p` hypothesis be deleted from the arithmetic lemma's signature.
Generalisable habit: when a prose route says "if `p ≡ a (mod q)` then …, and the other residue is
impossible", test the unconditional claim over `ZMod q^k` with `decide` before formalising a split.

**Approach considered and rejected.** The obvious reuse was
`FibonacciCovering.exists_entry_pow_congr_mul` (the directive names it). It **does not apply**:
it is stated only for bare exponents `c^n`, and the shifted tower is `c^n − 2`. Rather than
restate it entrywise for shifted exponents, I lifted the same mechanism to the level of `orderOf`
(`exists_period_orderOf_dvd`), where it composes directly with
`TheoremDGround.dvd_trace_sub_of_orderOf_dvd`, which already accepts arbitrary `a`, `b`. **Any
future work on a shifted tower should go through that door, not the entrywise one.**

**Assumption I operated under, and it is load-bearing:** `n ≥ 1`. At `n = 0` the exponent identity
`(3^(n+kj) − 2) − (3^n − 2) = 3^n(3^(kj) − 1)` is *false* in ℕ, because `3⁰ − 2 = 0` rather than
`−1`. Harmless under `atTop`, but do not lift the lemma to all `n`.
