## 🧠 Context to carry forward

The single load-bearing discovery, and the thing a successor should not have to re-find:

> **At `d = 2, a₀ = 1` Dubickas's substitution (4) makes the recursion EXACT.**
> With `y n = x n + a₁/2`:  `y (n+1) = y n ² − c`, where `c = (a₁² − 2a₁ − 4a₂)/4`.

The paper's `O(y^(d−2))` error term in (5) is a *constant* at `d = 2`. Three collapses follow, and
together they turned a ~5-lemma paper into two files:

- **Lemma 10 is unnecessary.** His "two polynomials agreeing at infinitely many `x_n` coincide"
  step exists only because *his* `y_{n+1} = Q(y_n)` is derived on a tail. Here the one constant
  `c` is fixed from the outset, so a tail forcing `y_{n+1} = y_n²` reads off `c = 0` directly.
- **Lemma 7 (Chebyshev) is unnecessary.** Replaced by a squaring identity: once
  `y_{m+j} = trace(β^(2^j))` exactly, with `S_N = Σ_{j≥2} β_jᴺ`,
  `T_N² = T_{2N} + 2β^N S_N + (S_N² − S_{2N})`, hence `c = 2β^N S_N + S_N² − S_{2N}`.
- **Conditions (17)/(18) are literally `c ∈ {0, 2}`** (since `4c = a₁² − 2a₁ − 4a₂`), so no
  (15)/(16) polynomial-identity casework.

Two more things worth keeping in mind:

- **The degree collapse reuses phase 6 for free.** The identity above makes the tail terms
  `O(|β₂|^(2N))`, hence bounded, hence `‖S_N‖ ≤ K β^(−N)` — *exactly* the decay hypothesis of
  `Mills.pisot_degree_bound` at **`μ = 1`**. Phase 6 happened to state that lemma generally in
  `μ`; that generality paid for itself a lap later. Keep doing that.
- **The final step needs no unit property.** Dubickas argues `β` is a *quadratic Pisot unit*
  (`ββ₂ = ±1`). Not needed: `c = 2(βγ)^N` holds for both `N` and `2N`, so `c/2 = (c/2)²` and
  `c ∈ {0, 2}` falls out of self-consistency across `N ↦ 2N`. This shortcut removed a whole
  conjugate-norm sub-argument.

Approach notes / dead ends avoided: I did *not* try to formalize Lemma 7 or Lemma 10, and did not
need `Dubickas2022PisotGap`'s `λ` explicitly (it is buried inside `pisot_degree_bound`). For

telescoping-tail proof is the right one.
